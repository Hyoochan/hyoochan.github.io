# Experimentation : making electromagnet

## 1. Materials
* Power Supply Unit
* Enameled wire
* Wire
* Hex key


## 2. Why
> I learned about Electricity at school and I bought a PSU and a DMM. So I wanted to do some projects and here it is.

## 3. Procedure

First, I wrapped **enameled wire** tightly around a hex key.

![hex key wrapped by enameled wire](/assets/img/enameled.jpg)


Second, I striped the coating off both ends of the enameled wire.


Lastly, I connected enameled wire at the power supply unit.
