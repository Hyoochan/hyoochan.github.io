# Moving to OpenSCAD : Why I Left Freecad

## 1. Why I am leaving Freecad?
In October 26, 2026, I was planning to make something that is related to aerospace engineering (My favorite topic).
And just tried to make some model rocket with **Freecad**. 
When I was sketching, I was using **b-spline** to make **nosecone**. And a lots of error comes out.
I was solving that about 2 hours. But, I didn't find what's problem.
It was too hard to do something with Freecad. Because of my skill issue, and some additional problem of Freecad.

Therefore, I decided to find something else.

## 2. Why OpenSCAD?
At this point, you might wonder why I am not using **Fusion360**, **Inventor**.
Well, I **can't**. Before 2years ago, I installed Linux in my computer. And I bought a new laptop and installed Linux again.
As you know, Autodesk's software doesn't support Linux.
So, I found something else :
- Onshape
- OpenSCAD

Onshape was great choice, But I wanted to work at my Local environment.
Therefore, I choosed OpenSCAD.


## 3. What is OpenSCAD
According to OpenSCAD's website,
> OpenSCAD is not an interactive modeller. Instead It is something like a 3D-compiler that rads in a script file that describes the object
> and renders the 3D model from this script file.

It means, It is not a general modeller like blender,fusion360,inventor,thinkercad.
It require a script file to make 3D object. (It's cool)
